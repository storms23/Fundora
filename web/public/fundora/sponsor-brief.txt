Sponsor discussion brief

Fundora supports conditional stipends for a community training workshop. Each of up to ten enrolled participants has a separate award of 20 Testnet XLM. Testnet XLM has no real monetary value.

The sponsor funds an award with fixed terms. The recipient submits private evidence. An independent verifier checks the organizer's completion record and approves that evidence version. A separate sponsor signature authorizes payment to the enrolled recipient. After expiry, unpaid awards can be recovered by the original sponsor.

Fundora uses Next.js, Supabase, Soroban and Freighter. The hosted preview can run with simulated network operations. Live wallet-backed submission, approval and payout still require operator rehearsal.
